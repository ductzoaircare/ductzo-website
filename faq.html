Frequently Asked Questions

What is air duct cleaning?

It is the removal of dust, debris and buildup from the ducts, vents and registers of your heating and cooling system.

How often should ducts be cleaned?

Many homes benefit from cleaning every 3 to 5 years, or sooner with pets, allergies, renovations or visible dust from vents.

How long does it take?

Most homes take a few hours, depending on size and system layout.
